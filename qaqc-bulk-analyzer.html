How to use this tool

1. To Estimate Time: Enter the Total Project Design Hours in the box below and click "Calculate Review Time". This sets the timelines in the top boxes.
2. To Calculate Grade: Expand the "Calculate Project Score" section. Enter your duration given and error counts to calculate your exact grades.
3. To Find Deadlines: First complete Step 1 to set the duration. Then expand the "Date Calculators" section to determine when to submit or when you'll get it back based on the Expected R&C.

2. Detailed Scope Guidelines

The below guidelines help determine if updated onelines must be sent to TPP for review & approval. If the scope of work includes anything in the applicable list below, then it must be sent in for review.

Applicable – components INCLUDE:

  • Topology Change
    • New/Removal Substation
    • Adding/Moving Line Terminal
    • Bus reconfiguration
    • New/Reconductoring Transmission Line
  • Adding/Removing/Replacing equipment
    • Circuit breaker
    • Circuit switcher
    • Disconnect switch
    • Current transformer
    • Gas insulated bus
    • Substation conductor
    • Transmission line drop
    • Transmission line conductor
    • Wave trap
    • Fuse
    • Protective relays with phase CT inputs
    • Phase relay setting
    • Digital Fault Recorders (DFRs)
    • Phasor Measurement Unit (PMUs)
    • Metering devices with CT inputs
    • Transducers with CT inputs
    • Power transformer
    • Capacitor Bank
    • Reactor
    • Generator

Note: If the project scope is only listed in the not applicable list (not shown here), it does not need review.
